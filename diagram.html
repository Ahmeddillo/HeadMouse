Core Design Decision
Instead of solving movement and clicking through the camera (the root cause of false triggers in existing systems), HeadMouse uses a hybrid input architecture — camera owns movement, physical device owns actions.
DEAD_ZONE
8px
Min deviation to register movement. Filters micro-tremors.
SPEED
8.0
Base velocity multiplier. Tune this first.
SPEED_CURVE
1.4×
Exponential curve. Large moves get proportionally faster.
